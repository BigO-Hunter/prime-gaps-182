## What the row's math actually says

An admissible 39-tuple (39 integers such that for every prime p the set misses at least one residue mod p) of width 182 has infinitely many integer shifts containing ≥ 2 primes ⇒ H_1 ≤ 182. The proof needs distribution estimates for primes in arithmetic progressions (BV-level and beyond — hence the Type II/III companions) to make Maynard's sieve produce 2 primes in a shift. H_1 = liminf of consecutive prime gaps; "≤ B" = some gap ≤ B occurs infinitely often.
